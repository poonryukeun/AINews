    Feyn AI 发布 SQRL，一个文本转 SQL 模型系列，可在编写查询之前检查数据库¶

    Feyn AI Releases SQRL, a Text-to-SQL Model Family That Inspects the Database Before Writing a Query 摘要：Feyn Labs 发布了 SQRL，这是一系列文本到 SQL 模型，可在提交查询之前使用只读探针检查数据库。旗舰产品 SQRL-35B-A3B 在 BIRD Dev 上报告执行精度为 70.6%，领先 Claude Opus 4.6，并提炼成可自托管的 4B 和 9B 检查点。费恩邮报

    原文：Feyn Labs has released SQRL, a family of text-to-SQL models that inspect a database with read-only probes before committing to a query. The flagship SQRL-35B-A3B reports 70.6% execution accuracy on BIRD Dev, edging Claude Opus 4.6, and distills into self-hostable 4B and 9B checkpoints. The post Feyn 来源：MarkTechPost
